SnapLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

In short: SnapLink does not collect any personal information or send any data to external servers. Everything you save (links, notes, tags, and preview images) is stored only on your device.

1. Information We Collect

SnapLink has no account system and does not collect any personal information such as your name, email address, or location. The content you save in the app (web links, notes, tags, and thumbnail images) is stored only in your device's local storage and is never transmitted to anyone, including the developer.

2. Network Usage

When you save a link, the app connects directly to the website you saved to fetch a preview (the page title and a representative image). No data is sent to any SnapLink server or third-party analytics service. This communication happens only between your device and the website you chose to save.

3. Photo Access

When you set a thumbnail manually, the app uses the iOS photo picker. This feature receives only the single photo you choose; the app never accesses your entire photo library.

4. Third Parties and Tracking

SnapLink does not use advertising identifiers, analytics tools, or tracking SDKs, and does not provide or sell any data to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

All data is stored on your device only. You can delete individual items within the app, and deleting the app permanently removes all stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

SnapLink is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated policy and its effective date on this page.
