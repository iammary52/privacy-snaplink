Support for SnapLink on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email iammary52@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Send screenshots only if they do not expose private information.

Save your first link

Open a page in Safari, tap Share, and choose the SnapLink extension. If you do not see it, look under More/Edit. You can also open SnapLink, tap +, paste a URL, and tap Save.

Organize your library

Hold a card and drag it onto a sidebar tag. Tap a tag to filter your library. Use Edit Tags to add, remove, or reorder tags. Search can match titles, notes, URLs, and tags.

A preview is missing

Some websites restrict previews or require a login. Your link can still be saved. Open Details and try Refetch, or use Upload Image to choose a local cover from Photos.

Use SnapLink offline

Your saved notes and cached previews remain available offline. Loading new previews and opening websites requires internet access. SnapLink does not save full articles for offline reading.

Move to another device

SnapLink has no built-in library sync. Device transfers and backups depend on your Apple and device settings.
